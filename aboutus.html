About Paeds in a Pod Wellness Center

A specialised healthcare practice based in Pretoria, focused on the physical and mental well-being of children and adolescents. Established in 2018 by Dr Isaura Ribeiro.

Our Story

Paeds in a Pod was founded with one goal: to bring paediatric physical and mental healthcare together in one place. We saw that children were being treated the same way, but every child is different.


We provide specialised services including general paediatrics, newborn and developmental milestone check-ups, autism and ADHD support, learning difficulties, behavioural challenges, developmental delays, parent coaching, counselling and speech therapy.

What We Believe

"Life is hard enough, your child's mental and physical well-being should not be too. Different doesn't make you less, it makes you uniquely you."


  • Child-centred, not condition-centred
  • Neurodiversity-affirming care
  • Multidisciplinary team in one roof
  • Support for families, not just children
