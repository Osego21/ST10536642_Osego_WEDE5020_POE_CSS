PAEDS IN A POD

PAEDS IN A POD WELLNESS CENTER IS A SPECIALISED HEALTHCARE PRACTICE BASED IN PRETORIA, SOUTH AFRICA,
THAT FOCUSES ON THE HEALTH AND WELL-BING OF CHILDREN AND ADOLESCENTS. THE CENTER WAS ESTABLISHED IN
2018 BY Dr ISAURA RIBEIRO WITH THE AIM OF BRINGING PAEDIATRIC PHYSICAL AND MENTAL HEALTHCARE TOGETHER
IN ONE PLACE. THE PRACTICE TAKES A HOLISTIC APPROACH BY FOCUSING ON THE INDIVIDUAL NEEDS OF EACH CHILD
RATHER THAN TREATING EVERY CHILD IN THE SAME WAY.

THE CENTER PROVIDES A RANGE OF SPECIALISED PAEDIATRIC SERVICES, INCLUDING GENERAL PAEDIATRICS, NEWBORN
AND DEVELOPMENTAL MILESTONE CHECK-UPS, ASSESSMENTS AND DIAGNOSIS, AUTISM AND ADHD SUPPORT,
LEARNING DIFFICULTIES, BEHAVIOURAL CHALLENGES, DEVELOPMENTAL DELAYS AND PARENT COACHING.

IT ALSO PROVIDES COUNSELLING FOR CHILDREN, TEENAGERS AND YOUNG ADULTS, AS WELL AS SPEECH AND LANGUAGE
THERAPY TO ASSIST WITH COMMUNICATION, READING, SPELLING, SPEECH AND FEEDING DIFFICULTIES.
